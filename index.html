Why Did the Strategic Housing Development Process Affect Democratic Engagement in Ireland's Planning of Large-Scale Residential Developments between 2016 and 2022?

Francesca Sorensen Lewis
Featured Research · Undergraduate Dissertation

Housing, Governance & Democratic Engagement

An independent research project I planned and delivered end to end. It examines how Ireland's fast-track Strategic Housing Development (SHD) process changed who gets a say in major planning decisions.

9,867Words
2Case Studies
First ClassGrade

Using a qualitative comparative case study of Castlelands SHD (Balbriggan) and Taylors Lane SHD (Ballyboden), I analysed Inspector's Reports, Board Decisions, Planning Authority reports and a High Court judicial review with a thematic coding framework.

Key Findings
  • SHD did not eliminate democratic engagement, but fundamentally restructured how it operated.
  • Public participation remained available but was mainly consultative, with limited influence on outcomes.
  • Decision-making was centralised in An Bord Pleanála, reducing the role of locally elected representatives.
  • Accountability shifted from political oversight to legal oversight through procedural transparency and judicial review.

01

Show, don't describe

I couldn't describe the look I wanted in words. Finding a visual reference ended the design changes in one step. Prototypes and examples beat written requirements.

02

Content before design

The site improved most when real content arrived, like my photos, video, dissertation and references. Design should frame evidence, not replace it.

03

Privacy by design

Personal data turned up in unexpected places, like the student number on my dissertation cover. Checking every file before publishing is part of responsible delivery.

04

Iteration is change management

Six versions felt slow, but each one narrowed what I actually valued. Planned, reviewed change led to a better result than getting it "right" first time.

05

AI needs a leader

AI made the build fast, but every decision, correction and piece of content came from me. Like any team, it performs best with clear direction.
